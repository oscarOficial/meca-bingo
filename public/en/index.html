How it works

Paste a playlist

From Spotify, Deezer, Apple Music, YouTube Music or Amazon Music.

Choose the prizes

Line, two lines, corners, X, full card… or draw your own shape.

Friends scan the QR

Each phone gets a different card. No app, no account.

Play and call

Players tap what they hear and call the prize; the host's screen says VALID or NOPE.

Two ways to play

Choose it when you create the game, depending on where the music comes from.

Automatic

The music plays in the host's computer browser and the free extension marks every song by itself.

  • Spotify, Amazon Music, Deezer, YouTube Music or Apple Music web player
  • It can sound through a speaker or the TV connected to the computer
  • The host plays too: nobody sees the song names
  • Computer with Chrome, Edge or Firefox

Manual

The music plays from anywhere and the host taps each song on the screen when it plays.

  • Any app or device: phone, speaker, TV, records…
  • The host marks the songs, so the host doesn't play
  • Works on a computer, tablet or phone
  • No extension needed

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Do players need an app or an account?

No. They scan the QR or type the 5-letter code in any phone browser and write their name.

Can two players get the same card?

No. Each phone gets a different card, and keeps it if it reloads.

How many songs do I need?

More players need more songs, so not everyone wins at once: about 28–38 songs for the 3×9 card and 48–58 for the 5×5, depending on the number of players. If your playlist is short, the app tells you how many players it can take.

Can the host play too?

Yes, in automatic mode: the extension marks the songs and the host never sees their names, so the host can join from a phone like everyone else. In manual mode the host marks the songs and sees the names, so the host shouldn't play.

Can I host from my phone?

Yes, in manual mode: the music can come from any device and you tap each song as it plays. Automatic mode needs a computer, because the extension only works in a computer browser. Keep the bingo page open on your phone during the game.

How are calls checked?

Every marked song must have played, and the marks must complete the prize. Otherwise the host screen shows NOPE and the player sees which song was wrong.

Where is our data?

Everything stays in the host's browser; phones connect directly to it. Your photos never leave the host's computer.

Do I need the extension?

Only for automatic mode (songs marked by themselves, host can play) and to import Amazon Music playlists. Manual mode needs nothing.

Do I need a license to play music in public?

Streaming accounts are for personal use. For bars or public events, check the music licensing rules of your country.

How can I contact you?

Write to info@mecabingo.oscasais.es. Ideas, bugs and questions are welcome.
